The **Google Pay (GPay) mobile application** uses Google’s **Material Design 3 (Material You)** color system. It relies heavily on high-contrast surfaces, readable financial typography, and standardized payment-status indicators across both **Light Mode** (default) and **Dark Mode**.

---

### 1. Brand & Core Accent Colors

Used for the **Google Pay Logo**, **4-Color GPay G-mark**, **Rewards**, and **Promotional banners**.

| Element / Usage | Color Name | Hex Code | Visual Role |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **GPay Primary Blue** | Google Blue | `#0B57D0` / `#1A73E8` | Main action color, primary icons, and active tabs. |
| **GPay Logo Blue** | Alexandra Blue | `#3A81F1` | Blue segment of the Google Pay "G" mark. |
| **GPay Logo Green** | Retro Green | `#2DA94F` | Green segment of the Google Pay mark. |
| **GPay Logo Yellow** | Amber Yellow | `#FDBD00` | Yellow segment of the Google Pay mark. |
| **GPay Logo Red** | Vermillion Red | `#EA4335` | Red segment of the Google Pay mark. |
| **Rewards & Cashback** | Teal / Deep Green | `#00534C` / `#0D652D` | Background for Scratch Cards and Rewards Hub. |

---

### 2. Light Theme Palette (Default)

#### **Backgrounds & Containers**

* **Main Screen Surface:** `#FFFFFF` — Pure white background for transaction history and contacts list.
* **Contact Avatar & Recipient Cards:** `#F0F4F9` — Light gray-blue pill/card containers.
* **Search & Pay Input Bar:** `#F0F4F9` or `#F8F9FA` — Search bar background at the top of the app.
* **Bottom Sheet / Bank Card Surfaces:** `#FFFFFF` with a `#E3E3E3` subtle top border shadow.

#### **Typography & Text**

* **Primary Text (Names, Transaction Amounts):** `#1F1F1F` — Near-black for high financial readability.
* **Secondary Text (UPI IDs, Phone Numbers, Dates):** `#444746` — Medium slate gray.
* **Muted / Placeholder Text:** `#747775` — Search bar placeholder text.
* **Bank / Account Details:** `#5F6368` — Subdued metadata text.

#### **Buttons & Interactive Components**

* **Primary "Pay" / "Send" CTA Button:** `#0B57D0` (Solid GPay Blue) with `#FFFFFF` text.
* **Secondary Buttons (e.g., "Check Balance", "Request"):** `#F0F4F9` background with `#0B57D0` text.
* **Floating Action Button (Scan QR):** `#D3E3FD` (Soft blue container) with `#041E49` dark blue icon.
* **Active Filter Chips:** `#0B57D0` container with `#FFFFFF` text.

---

### 3. Dark Theme Palette

#### **Backgrounds & Containers**

* **Main Screen Surface:** `#121212` or `#131314` — Deep charcoal black.
* **Contact Avatar & Recipient Cards:** `#1E1F22` or `#282A2C` — Elevated dark gray cards.
* **Search & Pay Input Bar:** `#1E1F22` — Deep gray input container.
* **Bank / Payment Method Cards:** `#282A2C` — Dark surface containers for saved cards.

#### **Typography & Text**

* **Primary Text (Names, Amounts):** `#E3E2E6` — High-contrast off-white text.
* **Secondary Text (UPI IDs, Dates):** `#C4C7C5` — Light gray for secondary details.
* **Muted Placeholder Text:** `#8E918F` — Subdued gray placeholder text.

#### **Buttons & Interactive Components**

* **Primary "Pay" / "Send" CTA Button:** `#A8C7FA` (Light blue M3 tone) with `#041E49` (Dark navy) text.
* **Secondary Buttons:** `#282A2C` container with `#A8C7FA` text.
* **Floating Action Button (Scan QR):** `#0A305F` container with `#A8C7FA` icon.

---

### 4. Financial & Transaction Status Colors

These colors indicate transaction states across both modes:

| Status | Light Mode | Dark Mode | Usage |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Success / Completed** | `#146C2E` (Forest Green) | `#81C995` (Soft Mint Green) | Payment successful checkmarks & positive balances. |
| **Pending / Processing** | `#B26A00` (Amber Orange) | `#FDD663` (Soft Gold Yellow) | Processing transaction warnings and timers. |
| **Failed / Declined** | `#B3261E` (Crimson Red) | `#F2B8B5` (Soft Coral Red) | Failed transactions, bank errors, and alerts. |

---

### 5. Specific Feature UI Components

* **QR Scanner Viewport:** `#000000` semi-transparent overlay with a `#0B57D0` or `#FFFFFF` viewport border outline.
* **UPI PIN Keypad View:** `#F0F4F9` (Light) / `#1E1F22` (Dark) bottom keypad container.
* **Scratch Cards / Rewards Grid:** Gold accent (`#FDBD00`), Emerald (`#2DA94F`), and Royal Blue (`#3A81F1`) card covers.
